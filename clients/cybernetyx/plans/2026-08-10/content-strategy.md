## Differentiation

**Voice positioning:** Move the conversation from hardware specs to classroom intelligence — show what changes inside the lesson, not just on the panel.

**Content gaps to own:**
- Practical AI teaching workflows (not futuristic hype)
- Teacher-empowerment framing in a category obsessed with "smart" labels
- Integrated ecosystem story vs disconnected EdTech stack

**Underserved segments:** School management evaluating total cost of fragmented classroom tools; principals seeking teacher adoption, not just hardware installation.
