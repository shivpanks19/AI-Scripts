# Cybernetyx — Social Media Context

**Client:** Cybernetyx (EyeRIS Interactive Flat Panel)

**Run date:** 2026-08-10

---

## Identity

Cybernetyx builds intelligent vision-based interactive and AI platforms that bring intelligence into physical spaces. On social, the brand speaks as a **premium EdTech innovator** — confident, educational, and practical — positioning EyeRIS as the AI Smart Panel that transforms classrooms from "smart" to **intelligent**.

**Social handles:** @cybernetyx (Instagram), Cybernetyx (Facebook)

---

## Target Audience

### Primary (decision-makers)
- School owners and management
- Principals and academic directors
- Education group decision-makers evaluating smart classroom infrastructure

### Secondary (influencers)
- Teachers and department heads
- Coaching institutes and universities
- Smart classroom solution providers

**Geography:** India-first with global education technology credibility (80+ countries, approved scale claims).

## Voice & Tone

| Context | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Instagram feed | Bold editorial hooks, teaching-outcome focused, save-worthy insights |
| Facebook feed | Slightly longer context, institutional proof, demo-oriented CTA |
| Headlines | Contrarian category reframes — smart board → intelligent classroom |
| Body copy | Practical, teacher-empowering, feature-to-outcome translation |

**Voice characteristics:** Intelligent · Forward-looking · Educational · Technological · Human-centered

**Prefer:** intelligent classroom, AI-powered teaching, interactive learning, Bright AI, EyeRIS, teaching assistant

**Avoid:** revolutionary, game-changing, disruptive, AI replaces teachers

## Platform Configuration

### Instagram
- **Cadence:** 3 posts/week
- **Format:** Single-image dark editorial 1:1 only
- **Hashtags:** 5–8 per post, education + EdTech + leadership mix
- **CTA:** Request a Demo (soft) or save/share prompts

### Facebook
- **Cadence:** Mirror Instagram (same visual, adapted copy)
- **Format:** Single-image 1:1
- **Tone:** Slightly more institutional context
- **CTA:** Request a Demo with landing URL reference

---

## Content Formats

**Allowed:** `single-image editorial feed post (1:1, dark)` only

**Forbidden:** carousels, stat-hero KPI cards, phone mockups, dashboard splits, multi-slide formats

**Slug pattern:** `{topic-kebab}-editorial`

---

## Example Posts (draft)

**Positioning hook:** "Smart boards display content. Intelligent classrooms assist teachers."

**Bright AI angle:** "Input grade, topic, tone — Bright AI drafts the lesson plan. You refine and teach."

**Teacher hero:** "The teacher leads the lesson. EyeRIS handles the prep, the tools, and the collaboration."

---

## Anti-Patterns

- Generic AI brain graphics or robot imagery
- "AI will replace teachers" framing (forbidden)
- Invented ROI or learning outcome statistics
- Unverified market leadership claims
- Stock handshake or graduation cap clichés
- Urgency/scarcity language ("limited seats", "hurry now")
- Competitor bashing by name
- Pure hardware spec dumps without teaching outcome context
