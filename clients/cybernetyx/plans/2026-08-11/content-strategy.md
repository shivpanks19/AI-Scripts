## Differentiation

**Voice positioning:** Move the conversation from hardware specs and screen counts to classroom intelligence — show what changes inside the lesson, not just on the panel.

**Content gaps to own:**
- Category reframe: smart → intelligent (not just a label swap)
- Thought-provoking contrarian hooks for decision-makers
- Practical intelligence framing in a category obsessed with "more technology"

**Underserved segments:** School management evaluating total cost of fragmented classroom tools; principals seeking teacher adoption, not just hardware installation.
