## Identity

Cybernetyx builds intelligent technology for physical spaces — EyeRIS for classrooms, BrightClass for the teaching ecosystem. On social, the brand speaks as a **premium EdTech category thinker** — intelligent, thought-provoking, confident, educational, and human-centered — positioning EyeRIS as the AI Smart Panel that moves institutions from smart boards to **intelligent classrooms**.

**Brand thought:** The future of education is not more technology in classrooms — it is making classrooms more intelligent.

**Social handles:** @cybernetyx (Instagram), Cybernetyx (Facebook)

---

## Target Audience

### Primary (ICP)
- School owners and management
- Principals and academic directors
- Education group decision-makers evaluating smart classroom infrastructure

### Secondary (influencers)
- Teachers and department heads
- Coaching institutes and universities
- Smart classroom solution providers

**Geography:** India-first with global education technology credibility (approved scale claims: 200,000+ classrooms, 15,000+ institutions).

## Platform Configuration

### Instagram
- **Cadence:** 3 posts/week
- **Format:** Single-image dark editorial 1:1 only
- **Hashtags:** 5–8 per post, education + EdTech + leadership mix
- **CTA:** Request a Demo (soft) or save/share prompts

### Facebook
- **Cadence:** Mirror Instagram (same visual, adapted copy)
- **Format:** Single-image 1:1
- **Tone:** Slightly more institutional context
- **CTA:** Request a Demo with landing URL reference

---

## Content Formats

**Allowed:** `single-image editorial feed post (1:1, dark)` only

**Forbidden:** carousels, stat-hero KPI cards, phone mockups, dashboard splits, multi-slide formats

**Slug pattern:** `{topic-kebab}-editorial`

## Anti-Patterns

- Generic AI brain graphics or robot imagery
- "AI will replace teachers" framing (forbidden)
- Invented ROI or learning outcome statistics
- Unverified market leadership claims
- Stock handshake or graduation cap clichés
- Urgency/scarcity language ("limited seats", "hurry now")
- Competitor bashing by name
- Pure hardware spec dumps without teaching outcome context
