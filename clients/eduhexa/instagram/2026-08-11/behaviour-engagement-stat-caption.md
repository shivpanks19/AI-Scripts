Before the new term even starts, the worry list is already set.

In educator surveys this season, behaviour and engagement are topping concerns — ahead of budgets, politics, and staffing for many school leaders.

Roughly three in four educators expect behaviour to be a major challenge. Seven in ten expect engagement to stay difficult.

This isn’t a discipline rant. It’s a capacity signal:
→ Support roles cut while referrals climb
→ Larger classes, fewer intervention staff
→ Students arriving with weaker focus stamina
→ Families rescuing before natural consequences land

The question for your leadership team isn’t whether it’s hard. It’s whether your support structure matches the pressure you’re about to face.
