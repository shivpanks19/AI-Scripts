# EduHexa — Content Strategy (Instagram)

**Horizon:** Weekly · 3 posts  
**Updated:** 2026-08-08  
**Goals (assumed for dry run):** Awareness + authority + consultation enquiries

---

## Strategic intent

Position EduHexa as the **weekly intelligence source** Indian school leaders follow — not another admissions agency account. Instagram earns saves and DMs; Community Pulse and consultations convert depth.

---

## Content pillars & balance

| Pillar | Target % | Weekly slots (of 3) |
|--------|----------|---------------------|
| Education Intelligence | 40% | 1 (Mon editorial) |
| Classroom & assessment | 25% | woven into Mon/Fri |
| Admissions & parent trust | 20% | optional Wed/Fri angle |
| Leadership frameworks | 15% | 1 (Fri carousel) |

**Weekly mix:** 1 editorial hook · 1 data/stat · 1 framework/carousel

---

## Topic clusters

### Cluster A — Prove-It Era (Aug 2026 priority)
- AI makes answers cheap; competence is hard to verify
- Live-proof assessment, oral defence, in-class demonstration
- Parent honesty vs marketing certainty

### Cluster B — Behaviour & engagement crisis
- New-term survey signals (behaviour > budget)
- Support staff cuts vs rising referrals
- Focus stamina, learned helplessness

### Cluster C — Exam architecture (India)
- Rank vs competence bands
- Board marks vs entrance tests
- Institutional trust after NTA noise

### Cluster D — Admissions integrity
- Marketing promises vs classroom reality
- Enquiry quality, parent partnership

## Production workflow

1. Pull latest Community Pulse (`clients/eduhexa/research/community-pulse-*.md`)
2. Select 1 dominant signal → Mon editorial
3. Extract 1 attributable stat → Wed stat
4. Distill 3 leadership questions → Fri carousel
5. Run pipeline Phases 6–9 per `content-calendar.md`

---

## Risks & guardrails

- **Stat integrity:** Only use numbers from pulse research with attribution in caption
- **AI controversy:** Frame as leadership design, not policing students
- **Brand drift:** Always resolve colors from BRAND_DNA.json at render
