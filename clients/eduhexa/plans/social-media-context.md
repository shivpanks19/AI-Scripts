## Target audience

**Primary:** Principals, institute owners, admissions heads in India (Tier 1–3 cities)

**Psychographics:**
- Overwhelmed by AI-in-classroom debates
- Anxious about admissions season and parent trust
- Skeptical of generic edtech pitches
- Want peer-validated insights, not motivational fluff

**Scroll context:** Mobile, between meetings, 3–8 second hook window

## Content formats

1. **Dark editorial hook** — one provocative headline (Prove-It Era)
2. **Stat hero** — single survey number + one-line implication
3. **Leadership carousel** — cover + 3 questions + EduHexa strip
4. **Trend table card** — 2×2 signal grid (future variant)
5. **Proof grid** — outcomes / institution count (sparingly)

---

## Example posts (draft)

### Mon — editorial
**Visual:** “Proof moved.” / “Back to the classroom.”  
**Caption hook:** Students aren’t lazy — they’re rational. If AI answers faster, why build the skill? Your credibility now depends on what you can verify in class.

### Wed — stat
**Visual:** “3 in 4” / educators expect behaviour to be a top challenge this term  
**Caption:** Engagement and conduct are beating budget worries on principal surveys. The question isn’t whether it’s hard — it’s whether your support structure matches the pressure.

### Fri — carousel
**Cover:** “3 questions before admissions season”  
**Slides:** What do you verify in class? / What still depends on take-home proof? / How do you explain the gap to parents?

---

## Anti-patterns

- Opening with “We are India’s leading…”
- Fake urgency (limited seats, last chance)
- Unsourced statistics presented as universal truth
- Generic AI brain clipart
- Competitor bashing
- Wall-of-hashtags (>15)
- Product feature dumps without institutional context
- Light-mode creatives that break established black+blue feed aesthetic without reason
