## Brand foundation

Close the effort–outcome gap for founders and marketing leaders — growth that compounds because someone owns the vision with the same conviction they started with.

### Vision
A connected business ecosystem where B2B, FMCG, and education brands run predictable revenue systems — not vendor-driven deliverable churn.

### Mission
Build intelligent growth systems (strategy + performance + creative + data) that drive measurable business impact for 150+ brands across B2B SaaS, consumer/D2C, and education.

### Values
| Value | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| **Outcome ownership** | Metric-driven impact over activity reports |
| **Partner, not vendor** | Extended team embedded in client growth |
| **Systems thinking** | Growth OS — modular, accountable, compounding |
| **Creative + data marriage** | Innovation married to strategic measurement |
| **Founder empathy** | Built by operators who wore five hats |

### Personality
Bold · Direct · Systems-minded · Purpose-driven · Anti-vendor · Operator-credible

### Brand promise
*Growth systems that move real outcomes — not another agency invoice.*

## Visual identity

### Color system (canonical — all creatives)

```css
:root {
  --background-dark: #0A0A0A;       /* primary social canvas — site theme-color */
  --primary: #FF5500;               /* Hexanovate growth orange */
  --primary-light: #FF8A3D;
  --primary-dark: #0A0A0A;
  --secondary-light: #FFB800;       /* highlight words, accent bar */
  --text-on-dark: #FFFFFF;
  --text-muted: #A1A1AA;
  --surface-card: #141414;
  --accent-glow: rgba(255, 85, 0, 0.22);
}
```

**Note:** Dark editorial posts use `primary_dark` `#0A0A0A` — not pure black `#000000`. Accent highlights use `#FFB800`; primary CTA orange `#FF5500`.

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Bold geometric sans (Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans)
- **Body:** Clean sans, high mobile readability
- **Style:** Contrarian editorial — large left-aligned headline, accent word, muted subline

### Logo
- **Wordmark:** HEXANOVATE / Hexanovate
- **Placement:** Bottom-right on social posters (~18% canvas width) — composite post-generation
- **Clear space:** Minimum logo height on all sides

### UI patterns
- Vertical accent bar left of headline (4px × ~120px)
- Subtle 2% film grain on dark canvas
- No stock handshakes, no generic AI brain clipart
- Abstract growth motifs only (arrows, nodes, subtle grid)

## Voice & messaging

### Voice characteristics
- **Contrarian clarity** — challenge "agency" framing directly
- **Operator direct** — short sentences, no fluff
- **Systems language** — growth engine, pipeline, outcomes, accountability
- **Warm confidence** — partner tone, not preachy

### Tone variations
| Context | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Instagram / Facebook | Punchy, scroll-stopping, save-worthy insight |
| Founder-facing | Empathetic — "we've worn your hats" |
| CTA moments | Direct invitation — consultation, DM, link in bio |

### Messaging framework
- **Tagline:** Growth Systems for B2B, FMCG & Education
- **Value proposition:** Intelligent growth systems that drive predictable revenue
- **Key messages:**
  1. We are not an agency — we are growth partners who own outcomes
  2. Effort without outcome alignment is the real gap
  3. Three vertical specialists under one growth philosophy

### Vocabulary
**Prefer:** growth systems, outcomes, pipeline, partner, HEXO, compounding, accountability, vision, revenue  
**Avoid:** synergy, thought leader, excited to announce, India's No.1, limited seats, game changer, revolutionary

## Imagery guidelines

### Prefer
- Dark editorial single-frame posts (1:1)
- Bold typography heroes with accent color on one phrase
- Abstract vector growth metaphors (nodes, loops, arrows)
- Contrarian headline stacks

### Avoid
- Carousel covers, stat/KPI cards, dashboard mockups
- Stock handshake photos, generic office teams
- Fake survey percentages on image
- Competitor logos
- Light template covers with numbered hooks

- Attribute case studies only when sourced
- Maintain distinction between Hexanovate parent and vertical brands (ThirdMeta, NativeUnit, EduHexa)
