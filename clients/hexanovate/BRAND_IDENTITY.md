| Value | Definition |
|-------|------------|

| **Proof over noise** | Case outcomes and pipeline clarity beat vanity metrics |

Strategic · Operator-grade · Confident · Human · Direct · Systems-minded

## Visual identity

### Color system (canonical — all creatives)

```css
:root {
  --background-dark: #0A0A0A;      /* primary social canvas — site theme */
  --primary: #0066FF;              /* electric royal blue — HEXA accent */
  --primary-light: #3385FF;
  --primary-dark: #0047B3;
  --text-on-dark: #FFFFFF;
  --text-muted: #A3A3A3;
  --surface-card: #141414;
  --accent-glow: rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.25);
}
```

**Note:** Hexanovate shares the HEXA blue system with EduHexa portfolio. Pipeline renders use Brand DNA tokens; `background_mode: dark` maps to `#0A0A0A`.

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Bold geometric sans (Inter, system-ui)
- **Body:** Clean sans, high readability at mobile scale
- **Style:** Corporate editorial — bold headline, tight subline, system/grid motifs

### Logo
- **Wordmark:** HEXANOVATE / Hexanovate geometric mark
- **Placement:** Bottom-right on social posters (~18–20% canvas width)
- **Do not:** Recolor away from primary blue; stretch; place on busy zones without scrim

### UI patterns
- Hexagonal motifs (footer clip paths on site)
- Subtle grid / circuit lines at low opacity
- Stat blocks and KPI cards for case-study posts
- Clean split layouts for B2B dashboard aesthetic

## Voice & messaging

### Voice characteristics
- **Operator-direct** — speaks to revenue owners, not vanity marketers
- **Systems-framed** — problems are architecture, not creative luck
- **Evidence-backed** — portfolio cases, not hype
- **Human close** — partnership and enquiry CTAs, not hard sell openers

### Tone by context
| Context | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Instagram / Facebook feed | Confident, punchy, save-worthy frameworks |
| Case study posts | Specific outcomes with client name when sourced |
| Thought leadership | Contrarian but practical — "not an agency" positioning |

### Taglines & key messages
- **Primary:** Not an Agency. A System.
- **Value prop:** One system instead of ten confused vendors and employees.
- **Outcome:** Predictable revenue. The kind you can actually plan around.
- **Vertical hooks:** B2B Growth Done Right · Consumer Growth, Engineered · Education Growth Solutions

### Vocabulary — prefer
growth system, revenue pipeline, compounding growth, predictable revenue, growth partnership, B2B growth engine, engineered growth, human intelligence

### Vocabulary — avoid
revolutionary, game changer, India's No.1, limited seats, hurry now, viral hack, 10x your leads overnight (without case attribution)

## Imagery guidelines

### Prefer
- Dark editorial layouts with blue accents
- Abstract system/grid metaphors
- Single dominant stat with sourced case attribution in caption
- Clean corporate splits — copy left, visual right
- Carousel covers with bold two-line hooks

### Avoid
- Stock handshake photos
- Generic AI brain clipart
- Invented survey statistics on image
- Competitor logos
- Orange/black template clichés from reference pins (use layout only)
