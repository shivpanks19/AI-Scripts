# Hexanovate — Brand Identity

**Source:** hexanovate.com + webhook brand brief ("marketing agency")  
**Updated:** 2026-08-08  
**Agency type:** Growth systems ecosystem — B2B, FMCG/D2C, EdTech

---

## Brand foundation

### Purpose
Build the infrastructure of growth so founders can focus on vision — not stitching disconnected vendors, tools, and reports together.

### Vision
A connected business ecosystem where every part works in harmony to drive unstoppable, compounding growth.

### Mission
Accelerate brand growth through intelligent digital strategies, performance-driven marketing, and specialist vertical units — each built to dominate its market.

### Values
1. **Accountability without excuses** — When something doesn't work, we say so first. No blame, no deflection.
2. **Enablement first** — Systems run it; humans own the outcome.
3. **Honest always** — Effort without outcome is an expensive hobby.
4. **Excellence always** — The long game is the only game; success measured in years, not campaigns.

### Personality
- **Contrarian operator** — "You've seen agencies. You haven't seen anything quite like this."
- **Engineer-founder credibility** — Built by people who fixed their own growth gaps.
- **Outcome-obsessed** — Data over instincts; predictable revenue over vanity metrics.
- **Empathetic strategist** — Understands businesses because we watched brilliant ones struggle needlessly.
- **Long-game builder** — Infrastructure, not campaign churn.

### Promise
One partner. Every function. Zero gaps between strategy, execution, and outcome.

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Plus Jakarta Sans Bold — stacked, editorial, high contrast
- **Body:** Plus Jakarta Sans Regular / Medium
- **Display:** Oversized contrarian headlines; tight letter-spacing on wordmark

### Logo system
- Wordmark: **Hexanovate** — clean geometric sans
- Sub-brands carry vertical descriptors (ThirdMeta, NativeUnit, EduHexa)
- Clear space: minimum logo height on all sides
- Dark backgrounds: white or light wordmark; accent rule in secondary coral

### UI patterns
- Dark editorial feed posts with bold headline stacks
- Subtle gradient halos (coral at 8–12% opacity on dark canvas)
- Abstract geometric heroes — no stock handshakes
- Footer URL strip: hexanovate.com

## Voice & messaging

### Taglines
- **Primary:** Not an Agency. A System.
- **Secondary:** The Planetary Growth Ecosystem
- **Outcome:** Effort vs Outcome. We pick outcome.
- **CTA:** Book a Demo / Start The Conversation

### Vocabulary — prefer
growth ecosystem, compounding pipeline, outcome-obsessed, systems-powered, long game, predictable revenue, one partner every function, strategy that executes, human intelligence system powered

### Vocabulary — avoid
revolutionary, game changer, best agency, limited time, hustle culture, vanity metrics, generic "digital marketing" filler

### Messaging pillars
1. **Anti-agency positioning** — Systems over slide decks
2. **Effort → outcome** — Reports without results are hobbies
3. **Vertical depth** — B2B, consumer, education specialists under one roof
4. **Founder empathy** — Built from fixing our own growth gaps
5. **Proof without fake stats** — Case study patterns, not invented KPIs on social

## Imagery guidelines

### Prefer
- Dark editorial single-frame posts (1:1)
- Bold typography-led layouts
- Abstract growth/system metaphors (orbital, connected nodes, pipeline flow)
- Founder/operator tone — not corporate stock
- Case study callbacks in captions (not on-image fake metrics)

### Avoid
- Stock handshake photos
- Generic AI brain clipart
- Carousel covers and stat-hero KPI cards on feed
- Invented percentages on images
- Competitor logos
- Sci-fi robot imagery
