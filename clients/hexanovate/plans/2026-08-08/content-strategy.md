# Hexanovate — Content Strategy (Weekly)

**Horizon:** Weekly (3 posts)  
**Platforms:** Instagram, Facebook  
**Policy:** [single-image-post-policy](../../../skills/brand-social-creative-pipeline/references/single-image-post-policy.md)

---

## Goals

- **Awareness:** Position Hexanovate as growth ecosystem, not another agency
- **Leads:** Drive "Book a call" / consultation CTAs

---

## Content Pillars & Balance

| Pillar | Weekly slots | Ratio |
|--------|--------------|-------|
| Positioning & POV | 1 | 33% |
| Outcome & founder truth | 1 | 33% |
| Ecosystem / vertical | 1 | 33% |

---

## Topic Clusters

1. **Not-an-agency** — System vs vendor patchwork; planetary ecosystem
2. **Effort vs outcome** — Reports without revenue; owning the number
3. **Connected growth** — One partner, three vertical specialists

---

## Weekly Content Mix

| Day | Platform | Format | Pillar | Slug pattern |
|-----|----------|--------|--------|--------------|
| Mon | instagram, facebook | single-image editorial 1:1 | Positioning | `not-agency-system-editorial` |
| Wed | instagram, facebook | single-image editorial 1:1 | Founder truth | `effort-outcome-gap-editorial` |
| Fri | instagram, facebook | single-image editorial 1:1 | Ecosystem | `connected-growth-ecosystem-editorial` |

**Every slot:** dark editorial 1:1, one PNG, slug ends `-editorial`.

---

## Differentiation

- **Voice:** Contrarian but operator-credible — founder story, not agency hype
- **Visual:** Dark editorial feed (quality bar: contrarian headline on `primary_dark`)
- **Gap:** Most agency feeds use light carousels and fake stats — Hexanovate uses bold POV + honest CTAs

---

## Content Formats

**Only:** `single-image editorial feed post (1:1, dark)`

**Not planned:** carousels, stat-hero, KPI cards, phone mockups
