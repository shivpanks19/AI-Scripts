# Hexanovate — Social Media Context

**Last updated:** 2026-08-08  
**Platforms:** Instagram, Facebook  
**Source:** BRAND_IDENTITY.md + client.json

---

## 1. Identity

- **Brand:** Hexanovate — planetary growth ecosystem (not a traditional agency)
- **Handle:** @hexanovate (Instagram), Hexanovate (Facebook)
- **Role:** Growth infrastructure for B2B (ThirdMeta), FMCG/D2C (NativeUnit), Education (EduHexa)
- **Niche:** Outcome-obsessed marketing systems for founders stitching disconnected tools and vendors
- **Positioning:** You have seen agencies — you have not seen a connected ecosystem that owns the number

---

## 2. Target Audience

- **Primary:** Founders and CMOs at B2B SaaS, consumer brands, and education institutions (India-first, global-capable)
- **Struggles:** Tasks and reports without pipeline movement; multiple agencies with zero accountability; effort/outcome gap
- **Wants:** Predictable revenue, one partner across functions, strategy that executes
- **Sophistication:** Has hired agencies before — skeptical of pitch theatre; responds to operator credibility
- **Platforms:** Instagram for bold editorial hooks; Facebook for mirrored feed + slightly longer context

---

## 3. Voice & Tone

**Adjectives:** outcome-led, contrarian, credible, warm, systems-minded

**Use:** growth ecosystem, pipeline, long game, honest conversation, book a call, effort vs outcome, one system

**Avoid:** revolutionary, game changer, India's No.1, limited seats, hurry now, best agency

**Formality:** Semi-professional — founder-to-founder, not corporate brochure

---

## 4. Content Pillars

| Pillar | Share | Focus |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Positioning & POV | 35% | Not-an-agency, ecosystem, contrarian hooks |
| Outcome & proof | 30% | Case patterns in captions (not fake on-image stats) |
| Founder truth | 20% | Effort/outcome gap, engineer-turned-operator story |
| Vertical spotlight | 15% | ThirdMeta, NativeUnit, EduHexa angles |

---

## 5. Platform Configuration

### Instagram
- **Format:** Single-image dark editorial 1:1 only
- **Cadence:** 3 posts/week (Mon, Wed, Fri)
- **CTA:** Book a call / Start the conversation
- **Hashtags:** 5–8, end of post

### Facebook
- **Format:** Mirror Instagram creative + post copy
- **Cadence:** Same 3 slots
- **CTA:** Book a call — link in first comment if needed

---

## 6. Content Formats

**Allowed:** `single-image editorial feed post (1:1, dark)` only

**Forbidden:** carousels, stat-hero cards, KPI grids, phone mockups, dashboard splits

---

## 7. Example Posts (draft)

**Hook example:** "You have seen agencies. You have not seen a growth system that owns the number."

**CTA example:** "Book a 30-minute honest conversation — no pitch. Link in bio."

---

## 8. Anti-Patterns

- Invented statistics on images
- Carousel slide indicators ("Swipe →")
- Generic stock handshake imagery
- Urgency/scarcity language
- Light-background templates off-brand from dark feed
- Competitor name-drops
