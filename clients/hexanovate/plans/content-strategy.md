## Differentiation

| Dimension | Hexanovate | Typical agency feed |
|-----------|------------|---------------------|
| **Voice** | Contrarian operator, engineer-founder credibility | Generic inspiration + case study brags |
| **Visual** | Dark bold editorial — one frame, one idea | Light templates, carousels, stat cards |
| **Proof** | Outcome framing in captions; real case patterns | Invented metrics on images |
| **CTA** | Book a Demo — honest conversation | "DM us" without substance |
| **Gap served** | Founders who've been burned by report-heavy vendors | Surface-level "growth tips" |
