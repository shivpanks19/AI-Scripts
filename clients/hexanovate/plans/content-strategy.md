# Hexanovate — Content Strategy

**Horizon:** Weekly  
**Goals:** Awareness, leads  
**Platforms:** Instagram, Facebook  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-08

---

## Strategic intent

Hexanovate social content must do two jobs simultaneously:

1. **Awareness** — Position as a growth *system*, not another agency; differentiate via effort–outcome narrative and vertical depth.
2. **Leads** — Drive demo bookings and "Start The Conversation" form fills via save-worthy insight + clear CTA.

---

## Content pillars + balance

| Pillar | Share | Purpose |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Systems over vendors | 30% | Brand differentiation, ecosystem story |
| Effort → outcome | 25% | Contrarian thought leadership |
| Proof & portfolio | 20% | Credibility via sourced case patterns |
| Founder truth | 15% | Human connection, long-game trust |
| Vertical expertise | 10% | B2B / FMCG / Education routing |

**Weekly mix (3 posts):** 1 positioning/editorial · 1 insight/stat · 1 framework/carousel or CTA

---

## Topic clusters

### Systems over vendors
- One partner vs ten vendors
- Strategy–execution gap
- Growth infrastructure metaphor

### Effort → outcome
- Reports vs results
- Task factories vs outcome owners
- "Expensive hobby" founder truth

### Proof & portfolio
- SEO-led demand patterns (5× traffic themes)
- Funnel conversion lifts (sourced ranges only)
- CPL / engagement improvements

### Vertical routing
- B2B pipeline (ThirdMeta)
- Consumer scale (NativeUnit)
- Admissions & classrooms (EduHexa)
