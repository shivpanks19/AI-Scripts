# Hexanovate — Content Strategy

**Horizon:** Weekly (3 posts)  
**Platforms:** Instagram + Facebook  
**Goals:** Awareness, leads  
**Updated:** 2026-08-08

---

## Strategic intent

Build **awareness** through save-worthy system frameworks that differentiate Hexanovate from traditional agencies. Convert **leads** via partnership enquiry CTAs and bio link — never hard-sell in the hook.

## Differentiation

| Dimension | Hexanovate | Typical agency feed |
|-----------|------------|----------------------|
| **Frame** | Growth system / revenue architecture | Creative showcase |
| **Proof** | Named portfolio cases | Generic "results" |
| **Visual** | Dark editorial + blue HEXA system | Bright template grids |
| **CTA** | Growth partnership enquiry | "DM for rates" |
| **Vertical** | B2B + FMCG + Education unified | Single niche or generic |

**Content gap exploited:** Operators want vendor-consolidation and systems language — underserved on Instagram vs creative portfolios.

**Underserved segment:** Mid-market B2B SaaS and education operators in India evaluating agency fatigue.

## Performance guardrails

- Attribute portfolio stats in caption (ELMO, EduHexa, etc.)
- No invented survey numbers on image
- 20% flexible slot next week for breaking industry news
- Track saves and profile visits as primary awareness signals
- Partnership form submissions as lead signal
