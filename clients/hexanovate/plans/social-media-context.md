## Target audience

**Primary:** Founders, CMOs, and growth leaders at B2B SaaS, D2C/FMCG, and education institutions (India + global English)

**Psychographics:**
- Tired of agencies that deliver tasks and reports, not outcomes
- Juggling multiple vendors — ads, SEO, content, sales ops — with gaps between them
- Skeptical of "revolutionary" marketing speak
- Want a partner who owns the number, not just the deliverable
- Scroll on mobile between meetings; 3–8 second hook window

---

## Voice & tone

| Dimension | Guidance |
|-----------|----------|
| **Register** | Confident operator — direct, not arrogant |
| **POV** | "We fixed this on ourselves first" |
| **Emotion** | Empathy for founder frustration → reframe with systems thinking |
| **Proof** | Case study patterns in captions; no invented stats on images |
| **Sales** | CTA in last 20% — Book a Demo / Start the conversation |

**Sound like:** Engineer-founder who became a growth strategist briefing a peer  
**Not like:** Generic agency pitch deck, hustle-bro LinkedIn, or vanity-metrics brag

## Content formats

**Allowed (only):** `single-image editorial feed post (1:1, dark)`

**Forbidden:** carousels, stat-hero KPI cards, dashboard mockups, phone UI heroes

## Anti-patterns

- Opening with "We're excited to announce…"
- Fake urgency ("limited slots", "hurry now")
- Invented KPIs on feed images
- Generic motivational quotes without operator context
- Competitor bashing by name
- Carousel or multi-slide formats on feed
- Stock handshake / team-high-five imagery
