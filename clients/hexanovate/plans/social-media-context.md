## Target audience

**Primary:** B2B SaaS founders, FMCG/D2C brand heads, education institute owners in India and global SaaS

**Psychographics:**
- Fatigued by multiple vendors and unclear ROI
- Want pipeline predictability, not creative awards
- Skeptical of "agency" label — respond to systems framing
- Save frameworks for leadership / strategy meetings

**Scroll context:** Mobile, between meetings, 3–8 second hook window

## Anti-patterns

- Opening with "We're the best agency"
- Invented statistics without portfolio attribution
- Stock handshake / laptop-on-desk clichés
- Urgency/scarcity ("limited slots", "hurry")
- Competitor name drops
- Pure black #000000 when brand canvas is #0A0A0A (use Brand DNA)
