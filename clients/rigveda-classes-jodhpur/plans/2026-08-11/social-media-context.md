# Rigveda Classes — Social Media Context

**Run date:** 2026-08-11  
**Platforms:** Instagram, Facebook  
**Ref:** BRAND_IDENTITY.md

## Identity

Rigveda Classes is a Jodhpur-based coaching institute for NEET, JEE and school academics. Social content should feel like useful preparation insight for students — not admission posters or feature catalogues.

## Target Audience

- NEET and JEE aspirants in Jodhpur and Rajasthan
- School students (Classes 9–12) balancing academics and competitive prep
- Parents seeking structured, disciplined academic guidance

## Voice & Tone

Motivational but practical. Mentor-like, not preachy. Relatable student situations. Confident without hype. Educational first, promotional second.

## Content Pillars

1. **Smart preparation** — studying effectively, not just longer
2. **Concept clarity** — understanding over memorisation
3. **Consistent practice** — discipline, revision, mock tests
4. **Student motivation** — relatable exam-prep insights
5. **Local trust** — Rigveda Classes as Jodhpur academic partner

## Platform Configuration

| Platform | Format | Ratio | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------|-------|
| Instagram | Single-image editorial | 1:1 | Primary visual channel |
| Facebook | Mirror Instagram creative | 1:1 | Same asset + adapted caption |

## Content Formats

- Single-image dark editorial feed post (1:1) only
- Layout: `student-editorial-full` — logo, tagline, headline, body, callout, icon row, footer

## Anti-Patterns

- Feature grids, fee cards, guaranteed ranks
- Generic motivational quotes without preparation insight
- Fear-based pressure messaging
- Invented results or testimonials
