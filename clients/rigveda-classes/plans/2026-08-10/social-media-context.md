## Identity

**Brand:** Rigveda Classes — NEET, JEE and School Coaching in Jodhpur  
**Handle:** @rigveda_classes_jodhpur  
**Tagline:** STUDY SMARTER. STAY MOTIVATED. SUCCEED.  
**Role on social:** Trusted academic mentor sharing preparation insights students actually want to save — not admission posters.

---

## Target Audience

| Segment | Needs | Content angle |
|---------|-------|---------------|
| NEET aspirants | Concept clarity, consistent practice, exam strategy | Smart preparation, revision habits, doubt-solving |
| JEE aspirants | Problem-solving, time management, mock tests | Focused practice, understanding over memorisation |
| School students (9–12) | Balancing boards + competitive prep | Discipline, study systems, mentor guidance |
| Parents | Structured coaching, trusted local institute | Preparation philosophy, student-first approach |

**Geography:** Jodhpur and Rajasthan — local trust matters; national exam prep relevance.

---

## Voice & Tone

- Motivational but never preachy
- Intelligent and practical — mentor-like, not salesy
- Relatable student situations (pressure, consistency, confusion)
- Confident without guaranteed-result claims
- Educational first — coaching mention is secondary

**Do:** Use contrasts (hard work vs smart work, studying vs understanding).  
**Don't:** Guaranteed ranks, fear tactics, generic motivational quotes, feature catalogues.

## Platform Configuration

### Instagram (primary)

- **Format:** Single-image editorial feed posts (1:1, dark)
- **Frequency:** 3 posts per batch (weekly label)
- **Goals:** Awareness, engagement, saves
- **Hashtags:** 8–12 mix of NEET/JEE prep + local + brand
- **CTA:** Join Rigveda Classes / Start Your Preparation

### Facebook (mirror)

- Same visuals and copy as Instagram
- Slightly longer caption acceptable
- Same hashtag strategy

---

## Content Formats

**Allowed:** single-image editorial feed post (1:1, dark) — `student-editorial-full` layout  
**Forbidden:** carousels, stat cards, KPI grids, phone mockups, fee cards

---

## Example Posts (draft tone)

---

## Anti-Patterns

- Starting every post with "Join Rigveda Classes"
- Inventing ranks, selections or testimonials
- Generic stock-student smiles with books
- Fear-based countdown posts
- AI brain / hologram graphics
- Guaranteed selection language
