## Target Audience

- **Primary audience**: Growth owners and founders at SMB to mid-market — especially admissions directors, coaching institute heads, and sales leaders who run Meta lead ads and close on WhatsApp
- **Secondary audience**: Sales / marketing heads (mid-market) who need pipeline visibility, team accountability, and WhatsApp/call discipline without another tool
- **Tertiary audience**: B2B operators wanting the agentic playbook customized to their stack (consulting upsell)
- **Pain points**:
  - Revenue scattered across 5+ tabs (Meta Ads Manager, WhatsApp, spreadsheet/Zoho, Canva for social, Google for reviews)
  - Can't answer "which ad produced last month's wins?"
  - Counsellors copy-paste instead of sell; leadership doesn't trust reports
  - Social posts made in Canva, published manually — disconnected from pipeline insights
  - Google reviews answered late or off-brand because GMB lives in another tab
  - CRM is a to-do list, not a revenue system
- **Goals**: Revenue clarity without becoming the glue between tools; tie ad spend to closed deals; automate nurture, social, and reputation so humans close and approve
- **Sophistication level**: Intermediate — they know Meta ads, WhatsApp sales, and basic CRM; they don't need CRM 101
- **Where they hang out**: LinkedIn (B2B decision-makers), Instagram (brand discovery + EdTech/coaching operators), Meta ads ecosystem, Google Business Profile (local trust)
