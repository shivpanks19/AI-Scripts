Your Meta ads are working.

Your brochures are polished.

But admissions still stall — and the reason is usually hiding in a metric nobody tracks.

**Median first-response time.**

Most Indian schools and coaching institutes average 8–12 hours to reply to a new enquiry. Meanwhile, a large share of parents message after 7 PM — and shortlist whoever responds first.

I've seen teams spend ₹4L on lead ads, blame creative, and discover counsellors were replying 14 hours later from personal WhatsApp numbers.

No shared pipeline. No after-hours acknowledgement. No manager view of which leads went cold overnight.

The fix isn't always more budget.

It's:
→ One inbox for every enquiry source  
→ Instant acknowledgement after hours  
→ Source-tagged pipeline from ad to enrolment  
→ Counsellor tasks with visible follow-up aging

Before the next intake cycle, audit reply-time data — not just CPL.

What's your institute's honest after-hours response setup? Genuinely curious what's working in 2026.
