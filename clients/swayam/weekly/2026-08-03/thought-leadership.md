# Thought Leadership — 2026-08-03

## The admissions metric nobody dashboards

Every institute tracks enquiries. Few track **time-to-first-reply**.

Industry data consistently shows Indian schools and coaching centres averaging **8–12 hours** for a first human response — while a majority of families shortlist whoever acknowledges them first, often within the same evening.

This isn't a marketing problem. It's an operations visibility problem.

When enquiries sit on counsellors' personal WhatsApp numbers, managers can't see:
- Which leads went cold overnight
- Which counsellor has a 2-hour vs 14-hour median reply time
- Which Meta campaign produced enrolled students vs form fills

The institutes winning peak season aren't always outspending competitors. They're out-responding them — with a shared inbox, source-tagged pipeline, and after-hours acknowledgement that buys time until a human counsellor calls back.

Before your next intake push, audit one number: median first-response time by hour of day. The answer will tell you more than your CPL report.
