# Future of Education — 2026-08-08

**Title:** Routing Intelligence — When Assignment Becomes Automatic  
**Angle:** Readiness before technology adoption

---

Admission teams are about to face a choice they didn't have five years ago.

Keep routing leads the way they always have — morning batches, WhatsApp groups, whoever's free — or let the system assign instantly based on counsellor load, course fit, and response-time history.

The instinct is to protect human judgment. "Our ops manager knows which counsellor handles NEET parents best." That's true — and it's exactly the knowledge that should be encoded in routing rules, not repeated manually at 9 AM every day.

What we're seeing in early adopters:

- **Load-aware distribution** caps active leads per counsellor instead of dumping equal batches.
- **SLA clocks** start at assignment, not at first call — making the gap visible before leads go cold.
- **AI briefings** surface which new enquiries need immediate human contact vs which can wait for automated nurture.

The risk isn't over-automation. It's pretending manual assignment still works at 3× peak-season volume.

Pilot routing automation on one course or one branch before the next surge. Measure assignment time and zero-callback rate for 30 days. Then decide if human judgment belongs in the routing logic — or in the counselling conversation after ownership is clear.
