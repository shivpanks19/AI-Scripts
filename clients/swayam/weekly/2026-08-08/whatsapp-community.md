Here's a pattern we keep seeing in admission audits.

The marketing report looks healthy. Meta CPL is acceptable. Enquiry volume is up.

Then someone asks: *Of last week's 200 paid leads, how many got a counsellor owner within 10 minutes?*

Long pause.

Most teams can tell you lead count. Very few can tell you **assignment speed** — the minutes between "form submitted" and "counsellor notified with context."

Industry data suggests **15–30% of paid leads** never get meaningful first contact. Not because parents weren't interested. Because the lead sat in a queue — a morning spreadsheet, a WhatsApp group, an ops manager's to-do list — while the family moved on.

During admission surge, this gets worse. Response time that was one hour in March becomes six hours in May. Nobody announces the change. Leads just quietly leak.

**Discussion prompt for the group:**

How do new enquiries get assigned to counsellors at your institute today — round-robin in a CRM, manual morning batch, or "whoever checks the group first"?

Genuinely curious what's working when volume spikes 3× in peak season.
