## Executive summary

**Swayam** (Sanskrit: *self*, *autonomous*) is an agentic CRM that closes the loop from ad spend to revenue—not just lead storage and follow-up reminders. The agency sells **integration and outcomes**: we design, deploy, and run agentic workflows across ads, WhatsApp, email, CRM, and AI so every enquiry moves toward conversion with measurable impact on direct revenue.

**Brand promise:** *Revenue runs itself—until you need a human.*

---

## 1. Brand foundation

### Purpose

To give growth-minded businesses an autonomous revenue engine—so marketing spend, conversations, and pipeline work together toward conversion without manual glue between tools.

### Vision

Every business enquiry flows through one intelligent system: from the first ad click to WhatsApp reply, qualification, follow-up, and closed deal—with AI agents handling the repetitive work and humans stepping in only where judgment matters.

### Mission

We integrate ads, CRM, WhatsApp, email, and AI into a single agentic stack (**Swayam**), then operate it with clients until revenue impact is visible in the dashboard—not just in activity logs.

### Values

| Value | Definition | How it shows up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Revenue-first** | We measure success in conversions and revenue, not vanity metrics | Win/loss reports, Meta ROI, pipeline value—not just "leads added" |
| **Agentic by default** | Automation and AI do the work; humans set strategy | Stage automations, AI briefings, bulk WhatsApp, social poster generation, GMB reply drafts, MCP agents |
| **End-to-end ownership** | One stack from ad to close—no broken handoffs | Ads → capture → qualify → nurture → CRM → quote |
| **Transparent operations** | Clients see what agents did and why | Activity timelines, call logs, delivery reports, AI advice on leads |
| **Practical intelligence** | AI that counsellors and owners can act on today | Next-step AI, dashboard briefing, natural-language CRM chat |

### Personality

| Trait | Expression |
| --- | --- |
| **Confident operator** | Speaks like someone who runs the funnel, not just sells software |
| **Sharp & direct** | Short sentences; numbers and outcomes over buzzwords |
| **Technically credible** | Comfortable naming integrations (Meta, MSG91, MCP) without jargon overload |
| **Outcome-obsessed** | "50k MRR from 10 CRM clients" beats "innovative platform" |

### Brand promise

Revenue runs itself—until you need a human.

**Naming rule:** Lead with **Swayam** in product contexts; lead with **agentic integration** in agency/sales contexts.

### Positioning statement

**For** business owners and revenue leaders who are tired of CRMs that only store leads,

**Swayam** is the **agentic CRM** that runs ads, captures and qualifies leads, automates WhatsApp and follow-ups, and manages pipeline operations in one place—

**unlike** traditional CRMs and point tools that stop at reminders and manual follow-up,

**we** connect spend to revenue with AI agents and an integration agency that implements and operates the full stack.

### Target audiences

**Primary — Growth owner / founder (SMB)**
- Runs or oversees ads and sales
- Wants one system and someone to run it
- Cares about cost per lead and close rate

**Secondary — Sales / marketing head (mid-market)**
- Needs pipeline visibility and team accountability
- Wants WhatsApp and call discipline without another tool

**Tertiary — B2B operators (consulting upsell)**
- Want the same agentic playbook trained on their stack

### Competitive differentiation

| Traditional CRM | Swayam |
| --- | --- |
| Lead database + tasks | Revenue loop: ads → lead → nurture → convert |
| Manual follow-ups | WhatsApp templates, stage automations, AI next-step |
| Separate ads tool | Meta Ads insights inside CRM |
| Social posts in Canva + manual publish | Social AI Poster — brief → image, caption, hashtags → Instagram/Facebook |
| Google reviews in another tab | GMB Reviews — sync, AI draft, safety preview, publish from CRM |
| Reports on activity | Win/loss, source drilldown, follow-up aging, employee targets |

## 3. Visual identity

### Design principles

1. **Revenue dashboard, not admin panel** — charts, pipeline health, and KPIs feel primary.
2. **Agent presence without sci-fi** — subtle motion, status indicators, "agent completed" patterns; no generic robot brain clipart.
3. **Trust + velocity** — deep stable base colors with sharp accent for actions and AI highlights.
4. **Mobile-first credibility** — matches shipped responsive shell; touch-friendly CTAs.

### Color system

```css
:root {
  /* Primary — Intelligence & trust */
  --swayam-primary: #1E3A5F;
  --swayam-primary-light: #2D4F7C;
  --swayam-primary-dark: #0F2744;

  /* Secondary — Agentic / automation */
  --swayam-secondary: #0D9488;
  --swayam-secondary-light: #14B8A6;
  --swayam-secondary-dark: #0F766E;

  /* Accent — Revenue & conversion */
  --swayam-accent: #D97706;
  --swayam-revenue-light: #F59E0B;

  /* AI layer */
  --swayam-ai: #6366F1;

  /* Neutrals */
  --swayam-background: #F8FAFC;
  --swayam-text: #0F172A;
  --swayam-text-on-dark: #FFFFFF;
  --swayam-text-muted: #64748B;
}
```

**Accessibility:** White headlines on `#0F2744` — AAA. Teal accent `#14B8A6` on dark — AA for highlights. Muted `#64748B` for footer only.

### Typography

**Primary:** Plus Jakarta Sans — Semibold wordmark, Bold headlines, Regular body  
**Fallback:** Inter, system-ui, sans-serif  
**Mono:** JetBrains Mono (data, UTM codes)

### Logo system

| Variant | Usage |
| --- | --- |
| **Primary wordmark** | `Swayam` — Plus Jakarta Sans Semibold, letter-spacing −1% |
| **Product descriptor** | Subline: `Agentic CRM` — Regular, 45% size of wordmark, neutral-600 |
| **Icon mark** | Stylized **S** loop — ad → lead → convert cycle |
| **Agency lockup** | `Hexa Agents` + "Powered by Swayam" for integration engagements |

**Clear space:** Minimum height of the "S" icon on all sides.  
**Minimum size:** Wordmark 120px wide digital; icon 24px.

**Don't:** Stretch, add gradients to wordmark, place on busy photography without scrim, use legacy "Sensible CRM" in customer-facing materials.

## 5. Imagery guidelines

**Prefer:**
- Real dashboard screenshots over abstract stock
- Pipeline board, WhatsApp thread linked to lead
- Meta ads report in CRM, AI briefing panel
- Dark editorial social layouts with bold outcome headlines

**Avoid:**
- Generic AI brain clipart
- Handshake stock photography
- Oversaturated orange-only palettes
- Competitor LLM logos as hero icons
- Invented metrics or fake revenue numbers on mockups
- Sci-fi robot imagery
- Carousel covers, stat/KPI hero cards, phone mockups on social

**Agent presence:** Subtle motion, status indicators, agent-completed patterns — not sci-fi.
