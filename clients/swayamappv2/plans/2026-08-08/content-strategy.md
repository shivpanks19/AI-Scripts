# Swayam — Content Strategy

**Horizon:** Weekly  
**Platforms:** Instagram, Facebook  
**Goals:** Awareness, leads  
**Policy:** Single-image dark editorial 1:1 only — [single-image-post-policy.md](../../../skills/brand-social-creative-pipeline/references/single-image-post-policy.md)

---

## 1. Content pillars & balance

| Pillar | Weekly share | Topic clusters |
|--------|--------------|----------------|
| Revenue loop POV | 35% | CRM vs agentic CRM, spend-to-revenue gap, pipeline leaks |
| Product capability | 30% | WhatsApp nurture, Meta in CRM, AI next-step, social AI poster |
| Proof & outcomes | 20% | Education/services client patterns (caption only, verified) |
| Agency integration | 15% | Deploy on day one, managed ads, agentic workflows |

---

## 2. Weekly content mix

**3 posts/week** — Mon (awareness POV), Wed (product/leads), Fri (brand promise / differentiation)

| Day | Pillar | Angle | Format |
|-----|--------|-------|--------|
| Mon | Revenue loop POV | CRM stores leads — Swayam closes them | single-image editorial 1:1 |
| Wed | Product capability | WhatsApp replies vs pipeline context | single-image editorial 1:1 |
| Fri | Brand promise | Revenue runs itself | single-image editorial 1:1 |

All slots: `creative_template_ref` ends with `-editorial`. Slug pattern: `{topic-kebab}-editorial`.

---

## 3. Differentiation

**Voice positioning:** Confident revenue operator — speaks like someone who runs the funnel, not sells contact storage.

**Content gaps to own:**
- Ads + WhatsApp + CRM in one narrative (not three separate tool pitches)
- Agentic automation without sci-fi hype
- Agency deploys the stack — product alone is not the story

**Underserved segments:** SMB founders who run their own Meta ads and close on WhatsApp; education admissions teams (EduHexa proof point in captions when relevant).

---

## 4. Content formats

**Allowed only:**
- `single-image editorial feed post (1:1, dark)`

**Forbidden:** carousels, stat heroes, KPI cards, phone mockups, dashboard splits
