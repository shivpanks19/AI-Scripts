# Swayam — Social Media Context

**Last updated:** 2026-08-08  
**Platforms:** Instagram, Facebook  
**Source:** BRAND_IDENTITY.md + client.json

---

## 1. Identity

- **Brand:** Swayam — Agentic CRM (product) + Agentic AI Integration Agency (services)
- **Handle:** @swayamapp (Instagram), Swayam (Facebook)
- **Role:** Revenue operating system — ads, WhatsApp nurture, pipeline ops, AI agents in one stack
- **Niche:** Growth owners tired of CRMs that only store leads; need spend-to-revenue visibility
- **Positioning:** Your CRM tracks leads. Swayam grows revenue.

---

## 2. Target Audience

- **Primary:** SMB growth owners / founders running Meta ads + WhatsApp sales (India-first, global-capable)
- **Secondary:** Sales and marketing heads needing pipeline accountability without another tool
- **Struggles:** Ads in Meta, replies on WhatsApp, follow-ups in memory; reports show activity not revenue
- **Wants:** One system from ad click to closed deal; agency to deploy agentic workflows on day one
- **Sophistication:** Has used traditional CRMs — skeptical of "lead management" pitches; responds to operator credibility and revenue language
- **Platforms:** Instagram for bold editorial hooks; Facebook for mirrored feed + slightly longer proof context

---

## 3. Voice & Tone

**Adjectives:** confident operator, sharp, technically credible, outcome-obsessed, direct

**Use:** revenue loop, agentic CRM, pipeline, nurture, qualify, close, Meta ads in CRM, WhatsApp nurture, book a demo, start free

**Avoid:** revolutionary, disruptive, synergy, touch base, circle back, lead management solution, generic AI brain imagery

**Formality:** Semi-professional — operator-to-operator, not corporate brochure

## 5. Platform Configuration

### Instagram
- **Format:** Single-image dark editorial 1:1 only
- **Cadence:** 3 posts/week (Mon, Wed, Fri)
- **CTA:** Book a Demo / Start your free account
- **Hashtags:** 5–8, end of post

### Facebook
- **Format:** Mirror Instagram PNG; platform-specific post copy
- **Cadence:** Same 3 slots as Instagram
- **CTA:** Same as Instagram; allow one extra sentence of context

---

## 6. Content Formats

**Allowed:** `single-image editorial feed post (1:1, dark)` only

**Forbidden:** carousels, stat-hero cards, KPI grids, phone mockups, dashboard splits

---

## 7. Example Posts (draft)

## 8. Anti-Patterns

- Fake statistics on image creatives
- Generic "AI will change everything" without product specificity
- Competitor bashing by name
- Urgency/scarcity language ("limited seats", "hurry now")
- Carousel or multi-slide formats
- Handshake stock photography
- Sci-fi robot brain imagery
