## Differentiation

**Voice positioning:** Confident revenue operator—not SaaS feature list. Contrarian headlines that name the gap (storage vs conversion, ads vs pipeline).

**Content gaps:** Competitors show dashboards; Swayam shows the full revenue loop and agency setup.

**Underserved segments:** Growth owners tired of wiring Meta + WhatsApp + CRM manually.
