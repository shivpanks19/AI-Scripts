# Swayam — Social Media Context

**Run date:** 2026-08-24  
**Platforms:** Instagram, Facebook  
**Goals:** Awareness, leads  
**Source:** BRAND_IDENTITY.md + swayamapp.com

---

## Identity

**Brand:** Swayam — Agentic CRM  
**Descriptor:** Revenue on autopilot  
**Agency layer:** Hexa Agents (agentic AI integration)  
**Promise:** Revenue runs itself—until you need a human.

Swayam is not a contact database with reminders. It is a revenue operating system: Meta ads → lead capture → WhatsApp nurture → pipeline ops → closed deal—with AI agents handling repetitive work and humans stepping in where judgment matters.

---

## Target Audience

**Primary — Growth owner / founder (SMB)**
- Runs or oversees ads and sales
- Wants one system and someone to run it
- Cares about cost per lead and close rate

**Secondary — Sales / marketing head**
- Needs pipeline visibility and team accountability
- Wants WhatsApp discipline without another tool

**Tertiary — B2B operators (consulting upsell)**
- Want agentic playbook trained on their stack

---

## Voice & Tone

| Trait | Expression |
|-------|------------|
| Confident operator | Speaks like someone who runs the funnel, not sells software |
| Sharp & direct | Short sentences; outcomes over buzzwords |
| Technically credible | Names Meta, WhatsApp, CRM integrations without jargon overload |
| Outcome-obsessed | Revenue loop, conversion, pipeline—not vanity metrics |

**Prefer:** agentic CRM, revenue loop, conversion, pipeline, nurture, qualify, close, integration agency  
**Avoid:** revolutionary, disruptive, synergy, touch base, lead management solution

**CTAs:** Book a Demo (primary) · Start your free account (secondary)

## Platform Configuration

### Instagram (@swayamapp)
- **Cadence:** 3 posts/week
- **Format:** Single-image dark editorial 1:1 only
- **Caption style:** Hook → problem → Swayam solution → CTA → hashtags
- **Hashtags:** 6–8 tags — #AgenticCRM #RevenueOps #WhatsAppCRM #MetaAds #Swayam

### Facebook (Swayam)
- **Cadence:** 3 posts/week (mirrors Instagram creative)
- **Format:** Same PNG; slightly longer caption acceptable
- **Tone:** Same operator voice; less hashtag density

---

## Content Formats

**Allowed:** single-image editorial feed post (1:1, dark)  
**Forbidden:** carousels, stat-hero cards, KPI grids, phone mockups, dashboard-split heroes

## Anti-Patterns

- Fake stats or invented ROI on image
- Generic AI brain clipart or sci-fi robot imagery
- Carousel slide numbers or "Swipe →" CTAs
- Competitor LLM logos as hero icons
- Lifestyle stock with tiny unreadable text
- Over-promising fully autonomous sales without human review
