# Swayam — Trend Research Brief

**Run date:** 2026-08-24  
**Window:** 14 days  
**Method:** WebSearch + site analysis

---

## Key trends

1. **Agentic AI in sales ops** — B2B buyers expect AI that executes workflows (briefings, next-step advice, automations), not chatbots that summarize dashboards.
2. **WhatsApp-first nurture in India** — Admissions and services teams convert on WhatsApp; CRMs that don't link inbox to pipeline lose deals.
3. **Meta ads ROI accountability** — Growth owners demand source-wise conversion inside CRM, not exported spreadsheets.
4. **Revenue ops consolidation** — SMBs consolidating ads, messaging, and pipeline into one stack vs. point tools held together manually.
5. **Human-in-the-loop AI** — Positioning shifts from "AI replaces sales" to "AI runs repetitive ops until you need a human."
