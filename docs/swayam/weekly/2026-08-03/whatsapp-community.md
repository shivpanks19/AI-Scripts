Most admission teams we speak with have the same blind spot.

They can tell you CPL from Meta ads. They can tell you how many enquiries came in last week.

But ask this: *What was your median first-response time last Tuesday at 9 PM?*

Silence.

That's when the real leakage happens. Parents message after dinner. Students compare three institutes before breakfast. The team that replies first — even with a simple "Got your message, we'll call you at 10 AM" — often wins the conversation.

The institute with the better campus video? They're still drafting a reply.

**Discussion prompt for the group:**

What's your honest after-hours response setup today — auto-reply, on-call counsellor, or "we'll get to it Monday"?

No judgment. Genuinely curious what's working for coaching vs school admissions teams.
