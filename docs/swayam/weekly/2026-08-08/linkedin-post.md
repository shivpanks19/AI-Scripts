Your Meta CPL looks fine.

Your enquiry count is up.

So why aren't admissions moving?

We audited a coaching chain last month. ₹6L in ad spend. 900 leads. Healthy CPL on paper.

Then we traced individual leads backwards.

Roughly one in four never got a counsellor owner within the first hour. Some never got called at all.

They didn't die in the ad funnel. They died in the assignment queue — a morning spreadsheet, a WhatsApp group, an ops manager's batch distribution that took 4 hours on busy days.

The pattern repeats across institutes:

→ Marketing tracks CPL  
→ Ops tracks lead count  
→ Nobody tracks assignment time  

Industry data puts pre-callback leakage at 15–30% for teams without automated routing. During admission surge, response times slip from one hour to "we'll get back by evening" — and nobody announces the change.

The fix isn't more counsellors on day one. It's instant ownership: auto-routed by course and load, with SLA alerts when a paid lead sits untouched.

What's your honest assignment workflow today — CRM round-robin, morning batch, or WhatsApp group free-for-all?
