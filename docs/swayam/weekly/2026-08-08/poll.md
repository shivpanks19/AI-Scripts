**Topic:** Lead assignment workflow

---

**Question:** How do new admission enquiries get assigned to counsellors at your institute?

**Options:**

1. **Automated in CRM** — round-robin or load-based, counsellor notified instantly
2. **Manual morning batch** — ops manager distributes leads once or twice a day
3. **WhatsApp group** — whoever checks first picks up new enquiries
4. **Counsellor's personal number** — leads go direct, no central routing

**Follow-up comment (post after votes):**

No wrong answers — we're mapping what actually happens in peak season, not what the process doc says should happen.

If you picked 3 or 4, you're not alone. Most institutes we audit start there. The shift that moves the needle is usually making ownership automatic before volume spikes.
