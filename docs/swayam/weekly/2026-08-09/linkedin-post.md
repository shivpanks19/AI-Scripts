Your Click-to-WhatsApp ads are working.

Parents are tapping. Chats are opening. CPL looks fine.

So why aren't demos filling up?

We traced enquiries for a coaching chain last month. Healthy ad spend. Strong chat volume on paper.

Then we measured time-to-first-reply.

Many parents waited more than an hour for a meaningful response. Some got a generic greeting while messaging three competitors in the same evening.

Operator data puts the gap at up to **8× conversion** for teams that reply within five minutes vs those who wait an hour.

The parent already has WhatsApp open. Intent peaks in that window — not tomorrow morning when your counsellor sends the fee structure.

The pattern repeats:

→ Marketing tracks CPL and chat count  
→ Counsellors track demos and closures  
→ Nobody tracks the five minutes in between  

Meta CTWA campaigns penalise slow first replies. After-hours enquiries sit unowned. DNP leads never get a recovery message.

The fix isn't a longer brochure on first message. It's instant contextual acknowledgement, one qualifying question, and human handoff with full ad context — even at 9 PM.

What's your honest average first-reply time during peak season?
