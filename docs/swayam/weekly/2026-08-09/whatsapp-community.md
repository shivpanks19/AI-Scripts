Quick question for admissions teams running Meta Click-to-WhatsApp ads this season 👇

When a parent taps your ad and lands in WhatsApp — who sends the **first reply**, and how fast?

We've been reviewing operator workflows across coaching and school admissions. A pattern keeps showing up:

→ CPL looks healthy  
→ Chat volume is up  
→ But demos and enrollments are flat  

The leak is often in the **first five minutes**.

Industry data suggests institutes that reply within five minutes convert at **up to 8× the rate** of those who wait an hour. Parents are messaging 5–10 institutes at once. The first helpful reply wins the shortlist — not the best brochure.

Yet most teams still rely on counsellors who are on demo calls, in meetings, or simply offline after 7 PM.

Honest check: What's your average time-to-first-reply during peak season — under 5 minutes, under an hour, or "we'll call tomorrow morning"?

Drop your number (rough estimate is fine). No judgment — we're mapping where the cliff actually hits.
